2 types of receptors: ionotropic and metabotropic  
Ionotropic is short term - pulses last few 10s of ms
Metabotropic is long-term - Pulses last around an order of magnitude longer, few hundreds of ms
### Excitatory Synapses
2 neurotransmitters: NMDA and AMPA.  

### Inhibitory Synapses
The inhibitory effect is almost exclusively carried by a neurotransmitter called $\gamma$-aminobutyric acid, or GABA for short.
