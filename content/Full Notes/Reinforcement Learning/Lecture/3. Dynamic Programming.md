**A method for solving problems by breaking them down into subproblems, solving the subproblems, and combining the solutions to said subproblems**

DP is a very general solution method for problems which have two properties:
- **Optimal Substructure:** The principle of optimality applies - The optimal solution can be decomposed into subproblems
- **Overlapping Subproblems:** Subproblems recur many times; Solutions can be cached and reused
MDPs satisfy both these conditions.
- Bellman equations give recursive decomposition 
- Value functions store and reuse solutions

#### Policy Iteration:
**Policy Evaluation:**
- Apply Bellman expectation equation over all states until convergence
- $v_{k+1}(s) = \sum_{a \in \mathcal{A}} \pi(a|s) \left( \mathcal{R}_s^a + \gamma \sum_{s' \in \mathcal{S}} \mathcal{P}_{ss'}^a v_k(s') \right)$
- until you reach $v_\pi$  
**Policy Improvement:**
- After expectation, update the policy by acting greedily with respect to the newly computed value function
- $\pi' = greedy(v_\pi)$
